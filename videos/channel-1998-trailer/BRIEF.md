## Intent

Video 2 of 2: the "1 + 4 mix" trailer for African Household Drama. It keeps the Side-Eye hook and montage from video 1 (CRT cold open, Mummy's side-eye, the 12-second countdown, the title slam, four episode beats). Then it turns into a night of 1998 GTV: a channel change to the news (the ticker reports the family's business), an "And now, a word from our sponsor" slate, and an ad break where the game is the advert. The camera pushes into the CRT for the advert's CTA, "Play now at african-household-drama.web.app", and the set switches off at the end.
