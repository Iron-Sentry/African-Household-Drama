## Intent

Video 1 of 2: the game trailer for African Household Drama, a choice-driven family comedy set in Accra in 1998. The user asked for "a really strong hook and CTA", 1 minute long.

Chosen concept, "The Side-Eye": the kissing scene is the hook and the countdown timer is the trailer's spine. Cold open on CRT static, then Maria and Ricardo lean in on the telenovela, then Mummy's eyes slide slowly toward camera: "You have 12 seconds." A montage of each episode's danger moment, cut to the music, follows. The CTA is "Play now at african-household-drama.web.app".

Video 2 (a separate project) is the "1 + 4 mix": the same hook and montage, ending on a 1998 GTV ad-break where the game is the advert.
